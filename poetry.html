The Lantern Tree

Beneath the silver hush of dusk,
the lantern tree leaned low,
and every bloom held a tiny star
that glowed in secret rows.

A fox with velvet paws
came listening by the gate,
while moonbeams poured like honey
across the mossy slate.

For the sky-walkers

Fox and Frost

In winter's lilac silence,
the fox ran barefoot through snow,
leaving behind a trail of ember
for every heart below.

The frost made lace upon the pines,
and time itself stood still,
while dawn stitched gold into the dark
with a patient, gentle thrill.

Beneath the pines
🩷

The Rose in the Well

A rose grew deep in moonlit glass,
with roots like whispered vows,
and every drop that fell around it
turned to secret cowslips in the clouds.

A queen once leaned to hear it sing,
and all her fears grew light;
for love, like water, learns the way
to bloom in darkest night.

From the fairy well
✨

Wishes in the Rain

The rain came down in silver bells,
tapping the windows wide,
and every wish forgot its name
inside the river's tide.

Then a girl laughed up at heaven
with wild and wonder-bright eyes,
and the storm became a song of stars
that danced in her surprise.

After midnight
